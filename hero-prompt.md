# Hero illustration — generation prompt (v2: Complexity → Clarity)

For use with the image-generation tool of your choice (ChatGPT image gen, Midjourney, Gemini, etc.). Derived from the master Bulka.AI cubist visual identity, tuned for an enterprise-formal payments audience: no human figures, no nature symbols, restrained palette.

---

## The prompt

Create a horizontal, high-resolution (16:9) cubist geometric illustration for the brand **Bulka.AI** — an AI tool for payment operations teams. The image must read as **enterprise infrastructure**, not whimsy. No human figures. No nature. No sun, mountains, trees, plants, or animals. No cartoon faces.

**SCENE — strict left-to-right narrative:**

The composition tells one story in three zones:

**LEFT THIRD — chaos / unresolved complexity.**
Scattered angular fragments at varied rotations and overlaps — small and medium rectangles, trapezoids, parallelograms, irregular polygons. They represent scattered documents, regulatory pages, scheme-rule manuals, ticket queues, and reject codes. The shapes are fragmented, tumbling, disordered. Use muted, cool tones: deep blue, gray-blue, slate, with **occasional small accents of warm orange or pure red** to suggest alerts or unresolved items. A few of the fragments may contain thin black horizontal lines hinting at text or table rows. No actual text or numbers — abstract only.

**CENTER — the AI core.**
A single structured geometric polyhedron, the visual anchor of the entire image. Built from interlocking triangular and trapezoidal planes — icosahedron-like, crystalline. Deep blue and white planes with thin black structural edges that read as facets. The core feels solid, present, and intelligent. Not glowing. Not ethereal. A confident, architectural object. Slightly larger than any single fragment on either side. Around it: a few thin straight black lines may radiate outward like structural axes, but no halo, no glow, no soft light. The core is the bridge between left and right.

**RIGHT THIRD — clarity / resolved structure.**
The same fragments from the left, now resolved into clean, ordered, horizontal and stacked planes — a structured grid or layered stack. Calm, stable, aligned. Use lighter, brighter tones: white, ivory, pale-blue, with a single accent of **turquoise** or **warm orange** as a small confirmation indicator (a triangular tick or a small block). Everything here is parallel, balanced, and intentional.

**BACKGROUND:**
Pure white or near-white field (#FFFFFF or #FAFAF7). No horizon line, no ground line, no landscape, no skyline, no scenery, no border. The composition floats on the white field with no framing line.

**STYLE — STRICT:**
- Only straight lines and sharply broken edges. No curves anywhere. No circles or arcs unless built from straight segments.
- Flat uniform solid colors only. No gradients, no soft shading, no glow, no transparency, no drop shadows.
- Each shape filled with a single uniform color.
- Composition built through layered overlapping geometric planes — depth comes from overlap and color contrast, not perspective or shading.
- Composition is calm, balanced, intentional. Stable horizontal weight. Not chaotic in execution even though the left side depicts chaos.
- Mood: structural intelligence, quiet confidence, enterprise infrastructure clarity.

**PALETTE — restrained:**
Primary: **deep blue** (#1B3A8B), **white** (#FFFFFF), **black** (used only for thin structural edges and accent lines).
Secondary accents (use sparingly): **slate / gray-blue** for left-side fragments, **warm orange** (#F26B1F) and **pure red** (#E03131) as small alert dots on the left, **turquoise** (#1FC8C8) as a confirmation accent on the right.
Avoid the full master-prompt rainbow. Avoid bright yellow, vivid green, and large color blocks. The image must read as enterprise, not festival.

**ABSOLUTELY FORBIDDEN:**
Curves. Gradients. Soft shading. Glow. Drop shadows. Realism. Photographic textures. 3D rendering. Glossy effects. UI screenshots. Sketch lines. Hand-drawn wobble. Cartoon faces. Human figures of any kind. Trees, mountains, sun, plants, animals, sky, water, landscapes. Bright yellow as a dominant color. Decorative ornament. Stock-illustration style.

**The image must communicate, at a glance:**
> Messy payments work, structured by intelligent AI, into clear ordered operations.

The narrative left-to-right is the entire idea. No additional metaphor needed.
